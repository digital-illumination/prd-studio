# Glossary: Shift Swap Requests (PRD-2026-901)

Terms and abbreviations used in [prd.md](./prd.md).

## Document and pipeline conventions

- **[OPEN: id]**: a marker in the document text pointing at an unresolved
  row in the Open Questions register (§9). Not yet decided.
- **[CONTESTED: id]**: a marker in the document text over a statement a
  named party actually disputes. The matching register row's Status reads
  "CONTESTED, unresolved" until a human settles it either way.

## Domain and role terms

- **QA**: quality assurance; the persona lens that checks every behaviour
  carries a verifiable acceptance condition (see the PRD Studio persona
  library).
- **Eligible** (worker): see FB-3's own Definition block in `prd.md`; not
  redefined here to avoid two sources of truth for the same term.
